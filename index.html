About INDUX 5.0

INDUX 5.0 is a hackathon organized by Vikrant University, Gwalior, bringing together students, developers, innovators, researchers and technology enthusiasts to develop practical solutions to real-world challenges.

The hackathon provides an opportunity for participants to collaborate, innovate and build technology-driven solutions across multiple domains.

Who Can Participate?

  • Students and student teams
  • Developers and technology enthusiasts
  • Researchers and innovators
  • National participants
  • International participants

Participation is subject to the official eligibility criteria and hackathon rules.
