About the Hackathon

Innovators, academia & industry — together

INDUX 5.0 – 2026 is an International-level hackathon focused on “AI for Real-World Impact,” bringing together young innovators, academia and industry to develop practical, AI-powered solutions to real-world challenges.

The hackathon encourages innovation and collaboration across emerging technologies, empowering participants to build, innovate and create meaningful impact.

Our Vision

Intelligent solutions, meaningful impact

To foster AI-driven innovation, strengthen industry-academia collaboration, and empower young innovators to develop intelligent solutions that address real-world challenges and create meaningful impact.

Venue: Vikrant University, Gwalior (M.P.)

Build for Real-World Impact

We invite student innovators from across the globe to showcase their skills, creativity, and problem-solving abilities at INDUX 5.0–2026. Transform ideas into intelligent solutions, build impactful prototypes, and take your innovation from concept to real-world application.

Eligibility & Team Formation

  • Open to students from all disciplines and academic backgrounds, including Diploma, UG, and PG programmes.
  • Team size: 3 to 4 members (with 1 faculty mentor — optional but encouraged).
  • Interdisciplinary teams encouraged.
Registration Fee: ₹2,500 per team — payable only after shortlisting by the Online Interview Committee.

Why participate?

  • 24 hours on-site to build, refine and present your prototype.
  • Real problem statements shared by industry partners.
  • Jury of industry + academia experts.
  • No participation fee for online registration and first-round submission.
