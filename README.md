# Hackathon-Indux-5.0-2026-VU 
## Theme: AI for Real-World Impact
# International Level Hackathon 2026
INDUX 5.0 – 2026 is a International-level hackathon focused on “AI for Real-World Impact,” bringing together young innovators, academia, and industry to develop practical, AI-powered solutions to real-world challenges. The hackathon encourages innovation and collaboration across emerging technologies, empowering participants to build, innovate, and create meaningful impact. This initiative is designed to foster creativity, problem-solving, and interdisciplinary thinking while connecting students, startups, researchers, and industry leaders around real business and societal challenges.

# The INDUX mission: Turn bold ideas into useful, real-world impact.

# 🚀 Three-Stage Selection Process
**Stage 1 -** Abstract, PPT & Video Submission
Submit your abstract, PPT and 3-minute video pitch online.

**Stage 2 -** Online Interview & Team Screening
Online interview and team screening with the jury.

**Stage 3 -** Onsite 24-Hour Hackathon & Prototype Demo
Selected teams participate in the onsite 24-hour hackathon to build, refine and present their prototype.
